The clock value controls BOTH pitch and tempo (they're linked — the walker's Timer-W clock determines both). Higher clock = higher pitch AND faster playback. Try 100000 first; adjust until SND_CONFIRM (#0) sounds right (it's a short upward beep ~100-200 ms). Lowering volume helps with square waves.
